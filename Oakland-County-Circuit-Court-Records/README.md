# Oakland County Circuit Court Records

Primary court records from **FG Agency Lending LLC v. WDB Holding MI, Inc., et al.**, Oakland County Circuit Court, Business Court, Case No. **2026-222791-CB**, Hon. Michael Warren.

These records document the commencement and administration of the 2026 receivership involving nine TerrAscend-affiliated Michigan entities. The PDFs are public court records obtained from the Oakland County Circuit Court docket.

## Receivership Entities

2. Spartan Partners Corporation
3. Spartan Partners Holdings, LLC
4. Spartan Partners Services LLC
5. Spartan Partners Properties LLC
6. Spartan Partners Licensing LLC
7. AEY Holdings, LLC
8. AEY Capital LLC
9. Thrive Enterprises LLC
