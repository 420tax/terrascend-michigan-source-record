# SEC Exhibits

Primary TerrAscend Corp. financing and Michigan Exit documents contained in, attached to, or associated with TerrAscend's public SEC filings.

This directory separates the operative agreements used in the source analysis from the larger SEC filing package so that readers can review the relevant contractual record directly. The complete **TerrAscend Corp. Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026** is also retained here as the underlying SEC filing package.

## Supporting Financial-Statement Extracts

The directory also contains selected financial-statement extracts used to reconstruct the Michigan Exit from the June 2025 decision through the period immediately preceding the receivership. Dollar amounts below are in millions unless otherwise noted.

| Reporting Period | Extract | Figures Highlighted / Role in the Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarter ended June 30, 2025 | [Q2 2025 Form 10-Q — Discontinued Operations (Asset Impairment)](./2025%20Q2%2010-Q%20Discontinued%20Operations%20%28Asset%20Impairment%29.pdf) | Captures the financial condition of the Michigan disposal group at the outset of the Exit. The highlighted disclosures include Q2 Michigan revenue of **$6.190 million**, gross profit of **$1.883 million**, and pretax loss of **$40.834 million**, including a **$34.959 million PP&E impairment** recorded after the Exit decision. At June 30, the disposal group reported **$44.939 million of assets** and **$23.991 million of liabilities**, including **$23.290 million of PP&E**, **$8.386 million of inventory**, and **$11.947 million of operating lease right-of-use assets**. |
| Year ended December 31, 2025 | [2025 Form 10-K — Discontinued Operations and Cash Flows](./2025%2010-K%20Discontinued%20Operations%20and%20Cash%20Flows.pdf) | Shows how far the Michigan disposal group had contracted during the first six months of the Exit. By December 31, reported disposal-group assets had fallen to **$12.713 million** and liabilities to **$12.616 million**. PP&E had declined to **$6.222 million**, inventory to **$0**, and operating lease right-of-use assets to **$5.998 million**; accounts payable and accrued liabilities were **$3.636 million**, operating lease liabilities **$6.342 million**, and finance lease liabilities **$1.923 million**. The extract also supplies the annual discontinued-operations cash-flow data used with the interim filings to reconstruct quarterly Exit-period cash flows. |
| Quarter ended March 31, 2026 | [Q1 2026 Form 10-Q — Note 7, Discontinued Operations](./2026%20Q1%2010-Q%20Note%207%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20operations.pdf) | Documents the disposal group immediately before the May 2026 receivership. At March 31, reported assets had fallen to **$5.213 million** against **$6.881 million of liabilities**, including only **$2.038 million of PP&E** and **$3.040 million of operating lease right-of-use assets**. Accounts payable and accrued liabilities were **$3.805 million** and operating lease liabilities **$3.076 million**; the previously reported finance lease liability had fallen to **$0**. These figures are central to distinguishing the shrinking GAAP disposal group from the later legal receivership estate. |
| Quarter ended March 31, 2026 | [Q1 2026 Form 10-Q — Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows](./2026%20Q1%2010-Q%20Unaudited%20Interim%20Condensed%20Consolidated%20Statements%20of%20Cash%20Flows.pdf) | Provides the cash-flow bridge for the final full quarter before receivership. The highlighted discontinued-operations figures show approximately **$0.165 million of operating cash use**, **$1.195 million of investing cash inflow**, and **$0.200 million of financing cash outflow**, producing approximately **$0.830 million of net cash inflow** from discontinued operations for the quarter. The investing inflow is particularly relevant when read against the contemporaneous decline in Michigan PP&E, while the filing describes the discontinued-operations financing outflow as primarily related to a finance-lease termination fee. |

## Contractual Sequence

The documents should be read as a sequence rather than as isolated agreements:

**August 1, 2024 Loan Agreement → June 30, 2025 Initial Letter Agreement → September 19, 2025 Michigan Exit Letter Agreement → April 17, 2026 Pending Litigation Letter Agreement → May 1, 2026 Forbearance Agreement → June 23, 2026 Amendment No. 5 / Amendment No. 1 to Forbearance Agreement.**

The June 30, 2025 Initial Letter Agreement is preserved as Exhibit A to the May 1, 2026 Forbearance Agreement filed as SEC Exhibit 10.3. The September 19, 2025 and April 17, 2026 agreements are Exhibits B and C, respectively.

## Archival Note

These copies are maintained as research exhibits for convenient review of the public record. The Source Notes also identify the corresponding SEC filing or exhibit number where applicable so that each document can be traced to its original public filing.
